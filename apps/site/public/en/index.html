FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Need a walkthrough? Read the guides (in French) →

How do I get an account?

Sign-up is by invitation. Ask the person who told you about Laststream for a referral link: each account has 4.

Does Laststream provide movies and TV shows?

No. Laststream hosts no content: it is a player and catalog that read the extensions you configure yourself. You are responsible for what you add and for respecting copyright laws in your country.

Does my data follow me from one device to another?

Yes: playback progress, lists and settings are synced with your account.

Is there an app for iPhone, Mac or Linux?

A native app is planned. In the meantime, the web version installs like an app and works very well.

How are sources chosen?

The sources from your extensions are sorted by language, then by quality, then by size. Laststream automatically starts the best one that suits your current connection speed, and you can always pick another one from the list.

Do my Stremio add-ons work?

Yes. Laststream reads add-ons in the Stremio format: paste the URL of the add-on's manifest, and its catalogs and sources are added to the app.

Do I need an extra service to watch?

It depends on the extensions you configure. Laststream does not provide any such service: it's up to you to choose your sources and, if they require them, the accounts that go with them.

How does the kids profile work?

You create a kids profile alongside yours. It only shows content suited to its age, based on the age ratings available, it doesn't open extensions or settings, and you can only leave it with a code.

Are friends and watch party available?

Yes. Friends, messages, activity feed, shared libraries and watch party are available with an account, but not from a kids profile. Your library stays private by default and each participant watches with their own sources: nothing is shared between accounts.

Do I need an account to join a watch party?

No. A guest can join a watch party without an account, through the invitation link you send them. They watch with their own sources.

How are the apps updated?

On Windows, updates are automatic. On Android, the app offers you the new version and installs it in one tap. The web version is always up to date.
