Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

An interface built for the couch as much as for your phone, fast from the moment you open it.

  • A cinema-style home screen

    A large featured poster, rows to browse, trailers, collections and a clear page for each title.

  • Sources, sorted for you

    Sources are sorted by language, then quality, then size. You can see them all and pick one by hand if you prefer.

  • Automatic playback

    Laststream measures your connection and starts the first source that works. No manual testing, no interruptions.

  • Live TV and guide

    Switch between your channels, check the program guide and find what's on right now, in one tap.

  • A real built-in player

    Native mpv or libVLC player: choose audio tracks and subtitles, picture-in-picture and a mini player so you can browse without stopping playback.

  • Profiles and kids mode

    One profile per person and a kids profile protected by a code, with content filtered by age.

  • AI recommendations

    A daily list for each profile, based on what you've watched and liked, so you know what to watch tonight.

  • Viewing statistics

    A dedicated page per profile: time watched, favorite genres, monthly trends. Everything stays on your account.

  • Themes that fit you

    Several ready-made themes and a customizable accent color, for an app that feels like yours.

  • Multi-device sync

    Your playback progress, lists and settings follow you across PC, phone and browser.

  • Your extensions, your rules

    Laststream hosts nothing: you add the extensions you choose, and the app reads them with built-in protections.

  • Works with Stremio add-ons

    Your Stremio add-ons work: paste an add-on's URL, and its catalogs and sources get added.

  • Soon

    Watch party and friends

    Watch at the same time as your friends: synchronized playback, public rooms and shared libraries.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?

Sign-up is by invitation. Ask the person who told you about Laststream for a referral link: each account has 4.

Does Laststream provide movies and TV shows?

No. Laststream hosts no content: it is a player and catalog that read the extensions you configure yourself. You are responsible for what you add and for respecting copyright laws in your country.

Does my data follow me from one device to another?

Yes: playback progress, lists and settings are synced with your account.

Is there an app for iPhone, Mac or Linux?

A native app is planned. In the meantime, the web version installs like an app and works very well.

How are sources chosen?

The sources from your extensions are sorted by language, then by quality, then by size. Laststream automatically starts the best one that suits your current connection speed, and you can always pick another one from the list.

Do my Stremio add-ons work?

Yes. Laststream reads add-ons in the Stremio format: paste the URL of the add-on's manifest, and its catalogs and sources are added to the app.

Do I need an extra service to watch?

It depends on the extensions you configure. Laststream does not provide any such service: it's up to you to choose your sources and, if they require them, the accounts that go with them.

How does the kids profile work?

You create a kids profile alongside yours. It only shows content suited to its age, based on the age ratings available, it doesn't open extensions or settings, and you can only leave it with a code.

Are friends and watch party available?

They are being rolled out and are marked "Soon" on this page. When they open, your library will stay private by default and each participant will watch with their own sources: nothing is shared between accounts.

How are the apps updated?

On Windows, updates are automatic. On Android, the app offers you the new version and installs it in one tap. The web version is always up to date.
