Share your library

Your library is private by default. Let your friends see the titles you choose, or your whole library at once.

  • 1 min
  • Windows
  • Android
  • Browser

Before you start

  • A signed-in account, on an adult profile (kids profiles share nothing).
  • Titles in your library, and friends to see them.

Steps

On each screenshot, the yellow frame shows where to tap or click; the numbers give the order. The app is in French: the screenshots show its English translation.

  1. Open the “Explore” menu (1), then “My list” (2). On a phone, it’s the “My list” tab at the bottom of the screen.

  2. Each poster has an eye at the top right. Crossed out, the title is private.

  3. Tap the eye: it opens, and your friends can see the title. Tap it again to make it private.

  4. To change everything at once: “Share with my friends” (1) or “Keep everything private” (2). With the “Movies” or “Series” filter, only those titles change.

  5. Laststream asks you to confirm, then tells you how many titles your friends can see.

Good to know

  • Your friends see your shared titles in their Friends panel: they tap your name, then “See their whole library”. They can add them to their own library.
  • A title you add later stays private until you open its eye.
